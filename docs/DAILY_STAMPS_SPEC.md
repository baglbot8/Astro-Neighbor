## 2. Norm's stamp card (lead rulings)

* **A daily card of 5 small tasks, rotating each real-life day** (the device's local date). **Do any 3 to earn that
  day's stamp.** Tasks come from a pool of things the player already does: play a mini-game, finish a safari, take a
  Fine-or-better photo, buy or place a decoration, do a favour, talk to 3 neighbours, sell prints to Gloop, win
  Norm's quiz, visit 2 planets, photograph something new, try on an outfit, pick up 10 scrap, take a home album
  photo... Only tasks the player's progress can reach are offered (no jungle task before The Tangle is open).
* **A week is Monday to Sunday** (local). **5 stamps in a week = that week's prize**, claimed from Norm: a
  stamp-only decoration or outfit (never stardust or scrap). Extra stamps that week do nothing more. Unclaimed prizes
  wait. Missing a week loses nothing but that week's prize.
* **Prizes** are the catalog items with `source: "stamps"`, given in catalog order, then outfits; when the list runs
  out, the prize is a choice of any stamp item again (a second copy) so the card never goes dead.
* **Norm is the mascot.** He hands out the card and the prizes on the Commons, with fresh funny lines each day in
  his voice (he is "definitely a normal human"). A small **Stamps** entry (in the journal or a HUD chip) shows
  today's tasks, ticks, this week's stamps and the prize silhouette.
* Cozy rules: no penalties, no streak loss messages, no timers on screen beyond "new card tomorrow".
* Changing the device clock can cheat it; accepted.

# Story spine spec: photograph your way home

Status: the contract for the campaign-spine builders, written 2026-09-22 by the lead from the user's
ruling and a read-only map of the campaign code (target `.astro_scratch/safari6-0921/merge2`).
Builders do not edit this file; the lead amends it with the measurement that justifies a change.
This file **wins over `docs/CORE_LOOP.md` and `docs/BUILD_PLAN.md`** wherever they disagree about how
a ship part is earned.

## 0. The ruling, in the user's words

> "I would go with story 2, but I don't want to lose the mini-game quests for the neighbors so I
> want this photograph request to be the last thing they want before they give the part you need
> for the ship."

And the two complaints it answers, from the user's second play:

> "starting a new game and going right into safari with no context of why I'm doing it didn't make
> sense story wise"
>
> "the ship was showing as damaged in a new game? I thought we were going to get rid of that part of
> the story to repair the ship? are we now repairing the ship and also strengthening our
> telescope/camera?"

**The answer to the second:** yes, you are repairing the ship — that *is* the story, and the rusty
rocket at the start is correct. What changes is **how you earn each part**: the last thing every
neighbour wants is a photograph. The camera and film upgrades are shop purchases, not a second story
ladder. There is one ladder: five parts, each ending in a photo.

## 1. How the campaign works today (from the map)

* Five neighbours each run a **three-step project** — a mini-game, a find, and a placement, in
  varying orders (`zorp.gd`, `bolt.gd`, `fen.gd`, `grig.gd`, `vela.gd`).
* **One shared hand-over:** `ProjectSystem._hand_over_part` (`project_system.gd:840-851`) fires when
  the last step completes (`_complete`, `if i + 1 >= steps.size()`, `:832-833`).
* **Appending a fourth step moves the hand-over after it without touching `_hand_over_part`.**
  Appending is the only index-safe insert: existing saves and place steps refer to step indices
  (`found` marks, `marker:s1_m0`).
* Tiers (`campaign_data.gd:26-30`): Zorp and Bolt at 0 parts, Fen and Grig at 2, Vela at 4.
* The talk runs ask → (already met? complete now) → progress lines, or complete + done lines + part
  (`handle_conversation`, `:741-785`). A step is asked **720 game minutes after the previous one**
  (`step_unlocked`, `:613-619`).

## 2. The design

### 2.1 A fourth step: `photo`

Each of the five projects gains a **fourth, last step** of a new type, `photo`:

```
{"type": "photo", "sight": "<catalog id>", "grade": "Fair",
 "lane": "<lane id>", "from": "<world>", "to": "<world>", "hours": [h0, h1]}
```

* **Asked** like any step, 720 game minutes after the placement step, in the neighbour's voice, and
  the ask **names where and when in plain words** — "between your home and my world, in the evening".
* **Met** when the sky journal holds that sight at **grade Fair or better** (§2.4).
* **Completed** on the next talk once met: the neighbour looks at your journal, says their done
  lines, and **then** hands over the part through the unchanged `_hand_over_part`.
* **Already have it when asked?** The existing rule completes the step in the same talk
  (`:779-780`). **Keep it, and give it its own delighted lines** — "You already have one! You really
  do look up, don't you." A player who explored before being asked is rewarded, and the story makes
  the sky matter *retroactively*. This is a deliberate choice, not an accident of the code.
* **Have it, but only as a Smudge?** The neighbour is pleased you found it and asks for a clearer
  one — and says how: **hold the shutter and let it bloom.** The story teaches the shutter (spec
  `SAFARI_FLIGHT_SPEC.md` R9).

### 2.2 The five asks

Chosen against five constraints the map found: reachable at the tier it is asked; on a lane that
is a photo flight at that tier (photo trips never go *to* the Commons, `safari_transit.gd:52-55`);
**not** a hinted rare (they cannot be heard during a chain — §5); **not** one of the five warm-rim
sights that read as one picture (`docs/OPEN_ISSUES.md`); and themed to the neighbour who asks.

| neighbour | tier | sight | catalog name | pointed lane and trip | hours |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Zorp | 0 parts | `zorp_moon_rim` | First light on Zorp's little moon | the Lantern Lane, home ↔ Zorp | any |
| Bolt | 0 parts | `yard_lights` | Bolt's yard, still working at night | the Lantern Lane, home ↔ Bolt | **16:00-07:00** |
| Fen | 2 parts | `tower_swifts` | Swifts off Fen's towers | the Long Way Home, home ↔ Fen | any |
| Grig | 2 parts | `pebble_moons` | Grig's three pebble moons | the Chalk Run, Zorp ↔ Grig | any |
| Vela | 4 parts | `mirror_moon` | Vela's mirror moon | the Frost Road, Zorp ↔ Vela | any |

* Bolt's is the only one with an hour window, and that is on purpose: it teaches, once, that the sky
  changes with the time of day.
* **Distinctness gate:** Zorp's, Grig's and Vela's sights all draw as `moonrim`. The critic must show
  the three side by side and confirm a player can tell them apart. If `mirror_moon` fails, Vela's ask
  becomes `glass_tide` ("Vela's ice fog, coming in", same lanes) — decided by the frame, not taste.

### 2.3 Once asked, the sight is guaranteed

A named photo must not be a lottery. **While an ask is open, every photo flight on its pointed lane,
in either direction, departing inside its hours, carries that sight in its cast.** Off the pointed
lane, draw rates are unchanged. This is the same idea as the existing "heard" mechanic, made certain.

The open asks live in **one plain flag**, and every system reads and writes it through **one shared
helper the lead wrote and tested before the builders started**: `src/sky/photo_asks.gd`
(`class_name PhotoAsks`). Two builders re-implementing "does this trip carry that ask?" is how this
project has shipped two systems that disagreed before, so nobody re-derives the rule.

```
GameState.flags["photo_asks"] = {
  "<sight id>": {"by": "<npc id or 'professor'>", "lane": "<lane id, or '' for any>",
                 "from": "<world, or '' for any>", "to": "<world, or '' for any>",
                 "hours": [h0, h1], "one_way": <bool>, "day": <int>}
}
```

API: `open()`, `has(id)`, `get_ask(id)`, `add(id, entry)`, `remove(id)`, `in_hours(hours, hour)`,
`matches(entry, lane, from, to, hour)`, `for_trip(lane, from, to, hour) -> Array[String]`,
`any_to(to, hour)`. A neighbour's ask is two-way (`one_way` false): home <-> Zorp is carried going
out and coming back. The Professor's is one-way to the Commons (§2.5). Hours wrap midnight.
**Tested by the lead, 20 of 20:** two-way and one-way pairs, wrong lane, Bolt's 16:00-07:00 window
at 18:00, 03:00 (wrap), 07:00 (end, exclusive) and 12:00, `any_to`, remove, and a JSON save
round-trip.

### 2.4 What counts as "the photo"

**The journal page**, not a print. The map settled it: a journal record is permanent, is not used up,
carries the sight id and survives a reload; a carried print has no sight id and Gloop's table eats
every carried print in one tap (`print_table.gd:275-294`).

One fix is needed first: the journal's `best_grade` is the grade of the **sharpest** shot
(`sky_journal.gd:310`), not the best grade earned — and a grade mixes sharpness with the caught
moment. Add a **`top_grade`** per record (the highest grade ever filed, falling back to `best_grade`
for old saves) and a query `has_grade_at_least(id, grade) -> bool`. Grades are ordered
`["Smudge", "Fair", "Fine", "Gallery"]` (`safari_scoring.gd:35`).

### 2.5 The very first flight has a reason — the Professor asks, for the trip to the Commons

**User ruling, 2026-09-22:** *"yes the professor can give a task to start, but it should be only
available en route to the commons where you meet him."*

* Professor Comet's opening radio call (`intro_director.gd:629-651`; his existing lines at
  `:646-650` stay) adds one ask, in his voice: come and see him at the Commons, and on the way,
  snap him a lantern-fish.
* The ask is opened with `PhotoAsks.add("lantern_fish", {"by": "professor", "lane": "", "from": "",
  "to": "hub", "hours": [0, 24], "one_way": true})` — **any photo trip TO the Commons carries it, and
  no other trip does.**
* **The errand exception.** `safari_transit.gd:52-55` makes every trip to the Commons a one-tap
  errand, and that came from the user's own earlier rule, *"a quick errand must stay quick"*. Both
  rulings hold: **a trip to the Commons offers "Photo time!" only while an open ask points there**
  (`PhotoAsks.any_to("hub", hour)`). Once the Professor has his photo, every Commons trip is a
  one-tap errand again, exactly as today.
* The mode card names the ask on the trips that carry it: **"The Professor wants: Lantern-fish"**.
* **He takes it in person, at the Commons** — not by radio. On the first talk with him after the
  journal holds `lantern_fish`, he is delighted, pays for it, closes the ask, and says the line that
  sets up the rest of the game: **"Your neighbours will want pictures too, you know. Keep that
  camera handy."** Any grade counts; a Smudge gets his delight anyway and one gentle word about
  holding the button to let the picture bloom.
* Hook: `Conversation.run` routes visitor -> project -> favours -> small talk
  (`conversation.gd:42-128`). The Professor (`mayor_orbit`) has no project, so one call right after
  the greeting hands the talk to a new `src/campaign/professor_ask.gd` when his ask is ready.
* `lantern_fish`: rarity 1, every lane, every hour, slow and large — the gentlest sight in the game.

### 2.5b The first flight teaches the flight (flight spec R14)

**User ruling, 2026-09-22:** *"the game should have a tutorial where it should pause multiple times
to show you: that it's viewable in the distance; how to zoom in; how to hold and snap the photo; the
number of limited camera shots you have."* That lives in the flight's own files and is specified in
`docs/SAFARI_FLIGHT_SPEC.md` §11 (R14). It runs on the first photo flight, which this section makes
the trip to the Commons.

### 2.6 The journal points the way

The journal's unseen-page "Forecast ... over <World>" line points at a ground telescope that does
not exist in real play (`sky_journal.gd:719-726`). For an asked sight, replace it with the ask:
**"Zorp asked for this — fly the Lantern Lane between home and Zorp, any time."** For every other
unseen page, remove the dead telescope forecast rather than leave a line that lies.

## 3. Requirements and their gates

* **SP1 — the fourth step exists and hands over the part.** All five projects carry it; the part
  arrives only after it; `_hand_over_part` is unchanged. Gate: drive each neighbour through all four
  steps in a scratch save and show the part appears only after the photo.
* **SP2 — the ask says where and when.** Gate: every ask line names the trip and, for Bolt, the
  time, in words a player can act on.
* **SP3 — already-have and Smudge each get their own lines.** Gate: construct each case in a scratch
  save and read what the neighbour says.
* **SP4 — the guarantee.** Gate: at least 200 draws per ask on its pointed lane inside its hours —
  the sight is in **every** cast; and the same number of draws off the pointed lane shows the rate
  unchanged from `merge2` within the spread.
* **SP5 — the first flight has a reason.** Gate: a new game, straight through the intro, speaks the
  Professor's ask before the first pad use; the home->Commons trip then offers "Photo time!" and the
  mode card names the ask; a trip to Zorp or Bolt does **not** name it; the flight carries
  `lantern_fish`; talking to him at the Commons afterwards plays his thanks, pays, and closes the
  ask; and after that, a trip to the Commons is a one-tap errand again.
* **SP6 — old saves still load and still work.** Gate: a save with some projects done and one
  mid-chain loads, the done ones are never re-asked, and the mid-chain one reaches the photo step.
  The cut-short hand-over state (`:760-764`), which today hands the part over, must now ask for the
  photo instead of skipping it.
* **SP7 — nothing that counted parts breaks.** The finale, Norm, the range gate, the rocket's rust
  stages and the toast "project: n of 3 done" (`:830`, now "of 4") all still work.
* **SP8 — the voices are right.** Every new line sounds like the neighbour who says it, judged
  against their existing lines and `docs/CAST_VARIETY.md`. One or two short lines per box. Nobody is
  ever mean, and the player is never mocked for a blurry photo.

## 5. Found by the map, not fixed here — logged

* **Neighbours can never say their rare-sight hints during their own chain.** Hints only come
  through small talk (`conversation.gd:141-145`), which the project pre-empts on every talk
  (`:101-125`, `project_system.gd:741-785`). So the six hinted rares tied to the five neighbours stay
  unheard and can never appear (`safari_catalog.gd:1071`). This breaks a feature the user asked for
  by name ("the neighbours might give you hints at rare sightings"). It needs its own fix.
* **Pacing slips.** Each neighbour gains a 720-minute wait and a six-hour flight, so tier 2, Norm
  (who arrives at 3 parts) and favours (off for a neighbour until their part is handed over,
  `favor_system.gd:597`) all move later. Measure it after the build; do not pre-tune it.

## 6. Lead rulings after story wave 1 (2026-09-22)

**Results:** S1 THE STEP passed in round 1. S2 THE ASKS passed in round 2 (round 1 failed on voice:
Vela's new lines were Grig's template with the nouns swapped, one copied word for word; Grig's
smudge line judged the player — "Not good enough"; and the already-have talk did not read as one
conversation). S4 THE FIRST FLIGHT passed in round 2 (round 1 found a double payment on a reload
mid-thanks). S3 THE DRAW reached the cap at FAIL **on file ownership only** — see 6.1.

### 6.1 S3 is accepted — the ownership table was wrong

S3's round-1 builder changed nothing at all; round 2 built the guarantee and the `orbit_laundry`
fix, and its critic re-measured every SP4 gate independently — different seeds and hours, all five
asks open at once — and passed them. The one blocking item was that the `orbit_laundry` fix touched
`src/sky/safari_catalog.gd`, which §4 did not list for S3. **§4 assigned the fix to S3 and gave no
builder the only file that could hold it.** The change is one line — `orbit_laundry`'s `dir` from
`DIR_IN` to `DIR_ANY`, matching its lane-mate `scrap_shoal` — read and accepted by the lead.

### 6.2 Two lead fixes, applied at merge

* **The Professor paid in the wrong currency.** `professor_ask.gd` paid `PROFESSOR_PAY = 15`
  **scrap**, justified as "one above a Fair print's price of 14" — but prints are priced in
  **stardust** (`print_table.gd:311`). Scrap is the story currency: each ship part costs 8 to fit,
  so 15 scrap handed a brand-new player nearly two fittings. Both the builder and its critic missed
  the unit. **He pays 15 stardust.** Exact inversion of a unit error; no number was tuned.
* **`PhotoAsks.any_to()` missed two-way asks** (found by S1's critic): it checked only `to`, while
  `matches()` also carries a two-way ask on the way back. Fixed in the lead's helper to "true when
  `matches()` is true for at least one origin", and re-tested: 16 of 16, including the critic's own
  failing case.

### 6.3 Ruling: the camera starts with the Professor's task

With S4 as built, a new player could still fly to Zorp or Bolt in photo mode *before* ever going to
the Commons — with no ask on board, and (once R14 lands) with the tutorial running on that flight
instead of his. That breaks both of the user's rulings at once: *"the professor can give a task to
start"* and the first-flight tutorial.

**While the Professor's ask is open, "Photo time!" is offered only on trips to the Commons.** His
radio call sends you there; that flight is the first photo flight, it carries the lantern-fish, and
it is the one the tutorial teaches on. Once he has his photo, every trip except the Commons is a
photo trip again, and the Commons goes back to a one-tap errand. An old save that is already past
the radio call never gets his ask, so nothing is gated for it.

### 6.4 Carried to the next story round (all small, all in S-owned files)

* **Smudge at the moment of asking** says the ask plus "(Your best is a Smudge. Fair or better,
  please.)" and only teaches *hold the shutter* on the next talk. Say the smudge lines in the same
  talk, so the lesson arrives when it is needed.
* **The route disappears.** When a Smudge is held, the status line gives only the grade, and a
  journal page filled below the grade shows no ask line — so a player whose Smudge came from another
  lane is never told the guaranteed trip again. Keep the route line in both.
* **Grig counts four moons.** `pebble_moons` draws four discs in a loose cluster; its catalog name,
  its blurb ("in a tidy row") and Grig's ask all say three. Grig is the character who counts
  everything. Fix the draw (`safari_shapes.gd`), not the words.
* **Two asks mislead about time.** Zorp's "My little moon, at first light!" sounds dawn-only when the
  data is any hour; Bolt's "evening into morning" can send a player out at 08:00-11:00, outside
  16:00-07:00. Make the words match the data.
* **Three smudge lines sound alike** — Grig, Fen and Bolt all open "Found it... Blurry, though", and
  Grig and Fen are asked at the same tier. Vary them.
* **The dev menu's "replay call"** re-opens the Professor's ask after he closed it and pays again.
  Dev-only, but it breaks that function's own contract. Guard it.
* **`showcase/nquiz_gating_probe.tscn` is stale** after S4 made the part-order questions follow the
  save: it reports 1495 mismatches against a quiz its critic proved correct. Update it or retire it.
* **The Professor's new lines** use " - " where his existing call uses an em dash; and on a first
  meeting with the photo already in hand, he introduces himself right before thanking you for a
  photo he asked for by radio. Reorder.
* **§6.3's gate** in `safari_transit.gd`.

**Accepted as built:** S1's extra toast "Zorp will want to see that photo!" (fires once per step);
the ask line beating a heard-hint quote on the same journal page; S4's modest 15 as a thank-you.
